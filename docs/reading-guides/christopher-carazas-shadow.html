Why it may be worth your time

Carazas uses his dog waiting at the door as a meditation on thresholds, trust, silence, and the possibility of returning to oneself. It is a gentle, intimate essay about presence rather than productivity, and it matches the questions Amir tends to save: how to feel less performed, how to inhabit attention, and how relationships can make a person feel anchored.
