Why it may be worth your time

Carazas examines the difference between polite feedback and real attention. Starting with uneven exchanges between writers, he asks what happens when generosity becomes unlimited availability and usefulness becomes a condition for belonging. It is especially relevant to Pursuit because it names the emotional bargain underneath publishing: not applause, but the hope that someone stayed long enough to understand.
