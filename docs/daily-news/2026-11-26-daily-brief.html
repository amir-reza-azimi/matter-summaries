US sets out new tariffs for 14 trading partners

Washington published proposed Section 301 tariffs for 14 economies, including China, the EU, Japan and India. Reuters reports potential rates from 20% to 40%, subject to caps and a January hearing; the BBC says the measures rely on a stronger legal route than the struck-down Liberation Day tariffs. They could still face legal challenge and trade retaliation.

US growth is revised higher while inflation remains elevated

US third-quarter GDP was revised to a 4.3% annualised pace, the fastest in three years, as consumer spending and business investment were marked higher. Reuters says October headline PCE inflation reached 3.8% year on year and core PCE 3.3%, reinforcing expectations that the Federal Reserve will keep policy tight.

Belgium puts NATO intern under electronic monitoring

A Belgian court ordered electronic monitoring for a Canadian NATO intern awaiting trial on espionage suspicions, Reuters reports. Prosecutors are considering an appeal. Authorities have not publicly identified the alleged third country, though Reuters sources linked the investigation to China. NATO previously said the case had not affected operational readiness, command structure or missions.

world · notable

Russia detains Murmansk governor in corruption case

A Moscow court placed Murmansk governor Andrei Chibis in pre-trial detention for two months in a major corruption investigation. Reuters says he is the first serving regional leader arrested on such charges since Russia's full-scale Ukraine war began. The case matters because Murmansk hosts Russia's Northern Fleet, Arctic port and icebreaker-supported sea-route infrastructure.

Lebanon prepares to open a second international airport

Lebanon plans to open Kleiat Airport near the Syrian border next month, with private investors funding its initial revival. The government expects flights to Turkey, Egypt and Jordan, seeking growth for the country's poorer northern Akkar region.

Boehly consortium signs deal for Lukoil's global assets

Todd Boehly said a consortium including the US development finance agency signed an agreement to acquire Lukoil's international assets in a share swap.
