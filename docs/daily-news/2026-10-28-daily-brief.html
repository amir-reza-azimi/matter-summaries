EU proposes a climate-resilience framework

The European Commission proposed a framework designed to move climate policy from disaster response toward earlier risk management. It includes a proposed climate-resilience law, common approaches for assessing risks, and tools intended to help public authorities, businesses and communities prepare for threats such as drought, wildfires and floods. The Commission also points to a future heatwave plan, climate-insurance alliance, water initiative and work to identify the continent's most vulnerable territories. The practical test will be whether these proposals become funded national and local measures rather than another layer of planning.

Commission seeks a larger and more operational Frontex

The Commission has proposed expanding Frontex's standing corps to 30,000 officers and giving the agency a more active role in EU-wide return operations. Its plan also includes a return coordination centre, a digital architecture for return and readmission cases, and an online monitoring and response centre with a quick-reaction capability. The package still needs agreement from the European Parliament and member states. It matters because the proposal would shift more operational capacity and data infrastructure toward an EU-level border agency, raising both implementation and accountability questions.

Cyclone Arnab makes landfall in Andhra Pradesh

Cyclone Arnab crossed India's Andhra Pradesh coast early on Wednesday after authorities moved tens of thousands of people from vulnerable areas. Officials set up more than 2,000 cyclone shelters across 20 districts and used mobile alerts to reach millions of residents, according to AP. The immediate focus is on casualties, power and transport disruption, and the condition of low-lying communities after landfall. The large evacuation effort is the important part of the story: it shows how emergency systems are being deployed before the full scale of damage is known.

A Washington grand jury declined a Justice Department request to indict former White House aide Cassidy Hutchinson, according to AP sources.
