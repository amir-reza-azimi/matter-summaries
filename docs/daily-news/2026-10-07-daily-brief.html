Ship captains receive $100,000 monthly premiums for Hormuz transits

Ship captains willing to transit the Strait of Hormuz are being offered salaries and bonuses reaching $100,000 a month, the Financial Times reports. The payments reflect the danger and operational difficulty of sailing through the strategic waterway while Iranian attacks keep crews and shipowners on edge.

The jump in compensation is a concrete sign that conflict risk is feeding directly into the cost of moving energy and goods. Higher maritime costs can pass through to freight rates, insurance and eventually consumer prices, even when physical supplies remain available.

Russian-backed forces help Mali retake the strategic town of Kidal

Malian forces, aided by Russian personnel, seized Kidal from Tuareg militia forces, according to the Financial Times. The town had been captured in April by secessionists and an al-Qaeda affiliate, making its recovery a significant territorial gain for the military government and its external security partners.

Kidal has symbolic and strategic importance in northern Mali, where state control has long been contested. The development underscores the expanding role of Russian support in the Sahel and the difficulty of separating local insurgencies, separatist politics and regional counterterrorism campaigns.
