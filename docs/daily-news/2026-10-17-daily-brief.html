A United Nations aviation assessment found Beirut airport lacks valid certification, an adequate emergency plan, and some regulatory capacity needed for long-haul operations, according to a report summary seen by Reuters. The findings are a setback for the effort to resume direct flights between the United States and Lebanon after a four-decade suspension. Lebanese authorities said corrective work is under way, but officials familiar with the process expect delays. The issue reaches beyond travel convenience: restoring service would be a visible diplomatic and commercial step, yet it cannot proceed responsibly without credible safety, firefighting, and oversight improvements.

United States import prices rose 1.1 percent in September and were up 8.1 percent from a year earlier, the largest annual gain since mid-2022.
