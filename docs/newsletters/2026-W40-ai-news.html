AI News Brief · 2026-W40

Week: 2026-W40 · Window: September 28–October 4, 2026

AI News Brief

Major: OpenAI made always-on agents available as Dots. The new agents can work between conversations using their own cloud computer, connected apps, and a user-defined goal. This matters because it turns a chat product into a persistent work surface, with permissions and review becoming the practical design constraints.

Major: Google introduced Gemini 4 Argon. Google positioned the model as its new frontier system, with the associated push focused on capability and competitive positioning. For AI-native commercial work, the useful implication is to keep model choice separate from workflow design: the operational advantage still comes from reliable context, tools, and evaluation.

Notable: Anthropic released Sonnet 5.5. The announcement positions it closer to Opus-level performance at a lower price. That is relevant for production agent and consulting workflows where a capable mid-tier model can improve unit economics without changing the broader control system.

Watch: Tavus introduced Griffin for interactive video agents. It aims to make real-time video conversations more natural. The important question is not the demo quality but whether the product can deliver a governed, high-volume customer or research workflow with clear consent and escalation paths.
