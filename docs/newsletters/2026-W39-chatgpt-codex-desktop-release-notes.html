September 22, 2026 · GPT-6 Sol and Luna in Work and Codex

What changed: OpenAI introduced GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna in ChatGPT Work and Codex. In Codex desktop, the app keeps the model you selected manually; available models and reasoning-effort options still depend on the plan and workspace.

Why it matters: This makes model choice an explicit, persistent part of a desktop Codex workflow rather than an automatic substitution. It supports deliberate routing between a faster general worker and stronger reasoning when both are available, while keeping the selected route inspectable.
