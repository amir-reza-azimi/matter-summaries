ChatGPT & Codex Desktop Release Notes · 2026-W39

Week: 2026-W39 · Window: 2026-09-15 to 2026-09-22

Two material desktop updates were published this week. Together they make desktop workflows more deliberate: authorized personal and work sources can coexist in one conversation, and Codex retains the model selected for that work.

September 17, 2026 · Connect multiple accounts to plugins in ChatGPT

What changed: ChatGPT now supports connecting multiple accounts to plugins, expanding earlier multi-account support beyond Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Contacts. The update is available across web, mobile, and desktop.

Why it matters: A desktop conversation can use intentionally authorized personal and work sources together, while each connection should still be reviewed deliberately.

September 22, 2026 · GPT-6 Sol and Luna in Work and Codex

What changed: OpenAI introduced GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna in ChatGPT Work and Codex. In Codex desktop, the app keeps the model you selected manually; available models and reasoning-effort options still depend on the plan and workspace.

Why it matters: This makes model choice an explicit, persistent part of a desktop Codex workflow rather than an automatic substitution. It supports deliberate routing between a faster general worker and stronger reasoning when both are available, while keeping the selected route inspectable.
