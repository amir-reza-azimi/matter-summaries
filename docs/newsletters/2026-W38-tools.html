Tool & Product Roundup · 2026-W38

Week: 2026-W38 · Window: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-14

This week’s useful tools cluster around practical AI operations: grounding agents in a large development corpus, turning real work observation into automation, and making marketing execution more measurable. The selection excludes generic launch recaps and previously rejected tools.

Firecrawl Developer Index

A searchable index of more than 70 million developer artifacts intended to give coding agents stronger retrieval context. It is worth a look for agent-assisted research and codebase workflows, where source discovery is often the bottleneck.

Caddi

An agent-building product that learns a workflow from a recorded example. The practical question is whether the observed-work approach can reduce setup effort for repeatable operations without creating opaque automations.

AskMyAds

A tool for querying and analyzing advertising performance data. Its relevance is direct for performance-marketing analysis if it can reliably translate questions into accountable campaign insights.

Recoura

A revenue-recovery product surfaced for solo founders. It is a useful example of a narrowly scoped agentic business workflow, especially for evaluating where automation can recover value rather than merely generate content.

Runable

A tool for AI-assisted business building and operational execution. It merits evaluation as a possible teaching example only if its workflows expose transferable steps rather than a closed black box.
