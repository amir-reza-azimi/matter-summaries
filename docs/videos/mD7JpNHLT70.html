Marketing Agents Masterclass (GROW your startup)

Channel: Greg Isenberg · Published: 2026-08-05 · Length: 44 min · Watch on YouTube

TL;DR

This is an unusually concrete marketing-agent walkthrough. One workflow treats LinkedIn engagement as an intent signal for outbound. The other turns calls, Slack, and internal expertise into a measurable, recurring content engine. Both are useful because they specify the inputs, enrichment steps, guardrails, costs, and feedback loops instead of treating an agent as a magic chatbot.

Walkthrough

The outbound system starts with ten to twenty creators or company accounts whose audiences overlap with the customer. Likes and comments become a stronger current signal than a static firmographic list. A scraper collects engagers, a Claude Code script deduplicates them, and a waterfall enrichment process moves from GitLeads to Apollo and then Origami or Prospeo before Million Verifier checks addresses. The result is a smaller, more timely prospect set rather than a large purchased list.

Infrastructure is deliberately separated. Burner domains and inboxes keep cold sending away from the main business domain, while cold email, marketing email, transactional messages, and core-company mail operate in distinct lanes. The stated operating cost is roughly $200 monthly for sending infrastructure and about ten thousand cold emails. The important principle is that deliverability and verification are part of the workflow, not an afterthought.

The content agent begins with human source material: sales calls, internal conversations, and team interviews. It extracts useful points, drafts posts in each person's voice, schedules them, then reads performance data. Winning themes are remixed on a roughly ninety-day cadence. That creates a closed loop between expertise, publishing, and learning rather than a generic AI content mill.

Standout moments

LinkedIn engagement is framed as a hand raise. The most transferable design choice is separating deterministic automation from places where the LLM needs to apply judgment.

Why this matters for my work

For JOIN marketing, the architecture is a useful reference for intent-led prospecting and expert-led content operations. For the 10x Operator, it provides a teachable example of an agentic commercial workflow with explicit data sources, human inputs, and quality checks. I could map one existing marketing process into this sequence and test the smallest viable loop first.
